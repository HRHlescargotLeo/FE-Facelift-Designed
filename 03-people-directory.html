Component 06 · Design layer

People directory

Layout is unchanged from the signed-off wireframe: prominent search above the results with suggestions appearing as you type, a persistent filter rail on the left, result count and active-filter chips, and cards carrying a one-line first-person summary.

Not in the current delivery baseline. The updated brief puts directory implementation out of scope, so this is designed ahead of a future phase rather than for the current build. It is included so the route decision covers the whole site rather than needing revisiting later.

Route A

Editorial rule

Closest to the site today

Rules rather than panels, square portraits on white, Merlot for the filter state and the search outline. Reads as a continuation of the current listing pages.

Route B

Colour block

Boldest

A Midnight Blue header band carries the title and search, then the results sit on white beneath it. Keeps the impact of the dark treatment without reversing a long list of names out of it.

Route C

Soft tint

Closest to the admired reference

Rounded portraits, a pill search field and a tinted filter rail. The closest of the three to the contacts pages Frontier flagged, and the friendliest for a page people will spend time scanning.

  • Rounded portraits are a bigger commitment than they look. They work with the studio cut-outs, but it means every future headshot has to be framed for a soft crop. Worth deciding alongside the rounded corners in the rest of Route C rather than separately.
  • Common to all three routes: filters are live — tick a few and watch the count and chips update — selection never depends on colour alone, checkboxes are keyboard-reachable, and “Show more” replaces the 34 pages of pagination the current directory uses.
  • Note on scope: because this sits outside the current baseline, treat it as a direction rather than a specification. If it moves into a future phase we should revisit the filter taxonomy with real usage data before building.
Route D

Broadsheet

Alternative composition

A dense index rather than a photo grid. Names set large in Steelfish, small circular portraits, expertise and office in aligned columns, and an A–Z jump. Four times as many people fit on screen, which is the right trade for a list 423 entries long.

Route E

Media card

Alternative composition

People as media cards — circular portrait on a flat tone, name in Steelfish caps, horizon rule carrying the office. Tone is driven by primary sector, so the grid doubles as a visual key to what each person works on.
