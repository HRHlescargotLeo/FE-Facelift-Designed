Component 02 · Design layer

Featured items

Layout is unchanged from the signed-off wireframe: three equal cards in view, one uniform 16:9 crop, category and date, title, summary, action, and arrow controls in the section header row.

Cards carry live content and imagery from the current “Latest thoughts” module. Each route includes one item with no artwork so the fallback can be judged rather than described.

Route A

Editorial rule

Closest to the site today

No card containers at all. A rule above each item and generous white space do the separating, which is how the current site handles its listings. The image and the type carry the colour.

  • 1The no-image fallback is a colour block at the same 16:9 crop, carrying the category name in display type. Nothing collapses, the row stays level, and the module still looks intentional when the aggregator returns an item without artwork.
  • Whole card is the link. One target, one tab stop, with “Read more” as an affordance rather than a separate link inside a link. This is the pattern the brief asks us to pick and stick to; it applies to all three routes.
  • Titles clamp at two lines and summaries at three on render. No editor-facing counters or validation, in line with the guardrail about not changing the content models.

Route D

Broadsheet

Alternative composition

Type leads instead of imagery. One lead story takes a large editorial headline, and the rest become a numbered index — the shape of a newspaper contents page rather than a card grid. Steelfish does the heavy lifting at a scale the current site never uses.

What would change in practice.
  • The 16:9 crop never changes between breakpoints, so a single rendition serves all three and the cards cannot go out of alignment.
  • Controls stay attached to the carousel in the section header rather than floating away from the track, which the brief asks for specifically.
  • Arrows now carry a disabled state at the start and end of the set, since the carousel does not loop. Try paging to the end — the next arrow dims and stops responding.
  • Touch targets on the arrows stay at 40px at all widths, and swipe would be additive rather than a replacement for the arrows if development confirms the library supports it.
